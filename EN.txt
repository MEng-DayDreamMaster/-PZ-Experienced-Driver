[h1]Main Features[/h1]
[list]
[*]Added a new Driving skill, which allows players to gain Driving XP by driving vehicles.

[*]Added a new non-occupation trait that grants 3 levels of Driving, 1 level of Mechanics, and 1 level of Electrical at the start. 
Driving skill XP gain is unlocked from the beginning.
This -4 trait is mutually exclusive with [b]“Sunday Driver”[/b], [b]“Claustrophobic”[/b], and [b]“Agoraphobic”[/b].

[*]The Driving skill must be unlocked by watching newly added VHS tapes.
[i]The VHS tapes and skill books will spawn normally at their corresponding locations throughout the game.[/i]
[/list]
[h3][u]No XP will be accumulated before the skill is unlocked[/u][/h3]

[h2]Skill Bonuses[/h2]
[list]
[*][h3]Increase Vehicle Top Speed[/h3]
Levels 1–10 provide a percentage-based bonus.
[i]For example, a Level 1 bonus of 0.08 results in a [b]top speed of 108%[/b] of the original value.[/i]
[*][h3]Improve Vehicle Braking Performance[/h3]
Same as above.
[*][h3]Reduce Vehicle Engine Noise[/h3]
Levels 1–10 reduce the attribute value by a percentage.
[i]For example, a Level 1 reduction of 0.03 results in [b]97% of the original[/b] engine noise.[/i]
[*][h3]Reduce Vehicle Part Collision Damage[/h3]
Reduces damage caused to vehicle parts by [i]active/passive[/i] collisions.
Same percentage-based scaling as above.
[/list]
[u][b]All of the above features can be individually enabled or disabled, 
and the parameters for each skill level can be freely adjusted in the Sandbox settings.[/b][/u]

[h2]Compatibility & Known Issues[/h2]
This mod can be safely removed from an existing save. 
If you encounter conflicts with other mods or any bugs, [url=https://github.com/MEng-DayDreamMaster/-PZ-Experienced-Driver/issues]please report them[/url].
[list]
[*]Known to conflict with other Driving skill mods.
[*]If you are using any mods that [b][u]override the UI tooltips[/u][/b], please place this mod [b][u]after[/u][/b] them in the load order.
[/list]
[h3]Compatible with [b]B42.20 and later[/b].[/h3]

[h3]The following known issues have only been observed in multiplayer. 
Please take note:
[/h3]
[olist]
[*][h3]Vehicle State Resets After Leaving the Game[/h3]
When quitting the game while sitting in the driver's seat, the vehicle's state may reset. 
The bonuses will not automatically be restored when entering the vehicle again.
[b]Solution:[/b] Simply re-enter the vehicle to restore the bonuses.

[*][h3]Original Skill Level-Up Banner[/h3]
After increasing a skill level, the notification banner may display an original field such as:
[quote]IGUI_perks_Driving[/quote]
This is a [b]base-game issue.[/b] When retrieving the skill's translation field, the game incorrectly concatenates getName() twice due to an error in getType().
[/olist]
